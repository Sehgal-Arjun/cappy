Hi! This is Cappy, a little cat that keeps your Mac awake.

To install:
1. Drag Cappy onto the Applications folder in this window.
   (Don't open Cappy from here; it needs to be in Applications.)
2. Eject the Cappy disk (click the eject arrow next to it in Finder).
3. Open your Applications folder and double-click Cappy.

The first time, macOS will say it can't check Cappy for malicious software,
because it isn't from the App Store. That's expected. To allow it:
4. Click Done.
5. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down.
6. Next to the message about Cappy, click "Open Anyway".
7. A second box pops up: click "Open Anyway" again, then enter your Mac
   password or use Touch ID.
   (The button only shows up for about an hour after step 3. If it's gone,
   double-click Cappy again and repeat.)

After that, Cappy opens normally. Look for the little cat in the menu bar
at the top right of your screen and click it.

- "Wake up" keeps your Mac awake. Pick how long with the buttons first.
- "Let it sleep" puts things back to normal.
- Turn on "Open at login" so Cappy is always there.

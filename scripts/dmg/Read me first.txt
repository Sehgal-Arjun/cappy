Hi! This is Cappy, a little cat that keeps your Mac awake.

To install:
1. Drag Cappy onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click Cappy.

The first time, macOS will say it can't check Cappy for malicious software
(it isn't from the App Store). That's expected. To allow it:
3. Click Done.
4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
5. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Cappy, then confirm.

After that, Cappy opens normally. Look for the cat in the menu bar at the top
right of your screen and click it.

- "Wake up" keeps your Mac awake. Pick how long with the buttons.
- "Let it sleep" puts things back to normal.
- Turn on "Open at login" so Cappy is always there.
